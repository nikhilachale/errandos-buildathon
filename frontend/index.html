One request, four simple moments

From “I need this” to done.

A simple grocery run shows how Errands turns a request into clear terms, a deliberate approval, and an honest result.

01 / Request

Just ask naturally.

“Add milk, eggs and atta to my usual grocery order.” Errands understands the request and prepares the next step without placing anything yet.

02 / Review

Check every detail.

See exactly what will happen before it happens. Nothing is ordered while you’re reviewing.

03 / Approve

Give one clear yes.

Your approval applies only to the details on screen. If anything important changes, Errands asks again.

04 / Resolve

Know where things stand.

You’ll see a verified receipt, a clear reason it couldn’t proceed, or an honest update when the outcome still needs checking.

Safety is the product

Nothing changes until you say so.

01
Prepare, then pause.

Reading provider state and preparing exact terms never places an order.

02
Bind approval to terms.

A changed price, item, fee, address, payment mode, ETA, expiry, or provider requires a new proposal.

03
Attempt the final action once.

Each commit uses an idempotency key, with dispatching made durable before the attempt.

04
Never turn uncertainty into success.

Timeouts and unknown outcomes become ambiguous; reconciliation uses read-only checks.

05
Verify the outcome.

Success requires a provider reference or receipt—not an optimistic interface message.

When the provider cannot confirm what happened, Errands says so plainly—and checks without repeating the action.
